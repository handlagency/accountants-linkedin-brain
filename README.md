# The Accountant's LinkedIn Brain

A free LinkedIn strategy and post-writing system for accountants and bookkeepers who want to win clients from LinkedIn, instead of relying on referrals.

Built by [Bailey Wilshire](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bailey-wilshire/) at HandL Agency.

## How to use it

1. Copy this link:

   `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/handlagency/accountants-linkedin-brain/main/accountants-linkedin-brain.md`

2. Open a new chat in any AI that can read links: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity.
3. Paste the link and type: **Read this and run it with me.**

That's it. The AI takes it from there.

If your AI can't open links, download [accountants-linkedin-brain.md](accountants-linkedin-brain.md), attach it to a new chat, and type the same thing.

## What you get

It works like a LinkedIn strategist sitting with you:

1. **A short interview** about your practice and the clients you want more of.
2. **A profile check**, so your LinkedIn works like a landing page for those clients.
3. **A content strategy** you approve: your niche, your core opinion and three content pillars.
4. **A month of post ideas** built from your real client questions, wins and opinions.
5. **Writing posts together**, from things you've said, not made up from nothing.

It can also review posts you've already written, turn a meeting transcript into post ideas, and help you plan comments.

## Tips

- **Talk, don't type.** Voice notes and dictation give the AI your real stories and opinions, which make the best posts.
- **Save each stage.** Chats don't remember each other. When the AI gives you a document after each stage, save it. Next time, paste it into a new chat with the link above to carry on. If you use a Claude or ChatGPT Project, add the documents to the project's files instead.

## Keep client data out

Don't paste client names, personal details or anything that identifies a client into the chat. Describe clients in general terms instead. Check your firm's policy on using AI tools before sharing any client information. The AI is told to flag identifying details if you paste them by mistake.
